How it works

Postcards get recycled.
Games get replayed — and shared.

Four steps from "who was that agent again?" to a referral machine that runs on delight.

1

Pick your profession

Realtor, lawyer, dentist, or insurance agent — our four launch industries each get four real games built around the adventures your clients actually live through with you.

2

Your client becomes the hero

They create their own character — name, look, style — and play the lead. You appear in-game as the guide NPC with your name, face and brand, showing up exactly when the hero needs you.

3

Clients play & share

A dopamine-rich adventure lands by text or email. Finishing unlocks a real perk — and a share loop that rewards both sides of every referral.

4

The CRM follows up

Every play is a signal. A past client replaying the home-search story? They're dreaming again. ClientQuest tells you who to call, when, and why.
