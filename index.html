How it works

Postcards get recycled.
Games get replayed — and shared.

Four steps from "who was that agent again?" to a referral machine that runs on delight.

1

Pick your profession

Realtor, lender, advisor, dentist, agent, attorney — each gets four games purpose-built around the moments your clients actually live through with you.

2

We brand it to you

Your name, headshot, brokerage, and colors woven into the story. You're the trusted guide the player becomes. Your sign is on the lawn when they win.

3

Clients play & share

A 5-minute, dopamine-rich story lands by text or email. Finishing unlocks a real perk — and a share loop that rewards both sides of every referral.

4

The CRM follows up

Every play is a signal. A past client replaying the home-search story? They're dreaming again. ClientQuest tells you who to call, when, and why.
